Maahi Ve Lyrics English Meaning: Highway | AR Rahman

Maahi Ve Lyrics English Meaning from Highway (2014), sung by A. R. Rahman, is a soulful ballad that reflects deep emotional longing and spiritual connection. Composed by Rahman with lyrics by Irshad Kamil, this song symbolizes a profound relationship between the two protagonists, Veera (played by Alia Bhatt) and Mahabir (Randeep Hooda), blending romantic and spiritual elements.
