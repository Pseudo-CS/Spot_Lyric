Nemurihime Translated Lyrics in English: Sekai No Owari

SEKAI NO OWARI drops a haunting ballad called Nemurihime. Fukase writes the lyrics, while the band and Yu Sasaki compose the music. The song tells the story of facing a future without a loved one.

Nemurihime Lyrics English Translation by Sekai No Owari

SEKAI NO OWARI describes a devastating scenario. The narrator imagines waking up one day to find his partner has fallen into ‘a deep, endless sleep’ (fukai nemuri ni ochite shimau n da ne), never to open their eyes again. He recalls their shared adventures, fighting dragons and finding treasure together, but now faces the terrifying possibility of walking through that same world alone.
